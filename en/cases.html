In-house

“Baili Liujiang” Digital Boat Companion · Culture & Tourism AIGC Interaction

An AIGC interactive project delivered for the "Baili Liujiang" river tourism experience in Liuzhou, officially launched in February 2026. The project reproduces the river scenery and its thousand-year cultural thread at pixel level; as visitors cruise the river, they meet historical figures from the landscape through digital interaction, turning a river trip into a conversational culture & tourism experience. The companion film features digital human "Baiye". The project was officially released by the Liuzhou Culture & Tourism Group Baili Liujiang Scenic Area.

Launched Feb 2026 Pixel-level recreation Immersive digital interaction

Officially released by client: "AI Renews Liujiang, a Digital Encounter Across a Thousand Years — Baili Liujiang Digital Boat Companion Goes Live"
