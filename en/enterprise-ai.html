FAQ

Enterprise AI: common questions

Why can't an enterprise AI project stop at a demo?

A demo only proves something is possible, not that it is useful. An AI project must start from a business scenario and be designed around data, knowledge, process, permissions, cost and collaboration. Entering real workflows, removing repetitive work and consolidating organizational knowledge is what delivers efficiency.

Can a company with unorganized data go straight to AI agents?

Not recommended. Documents, processes and cases that have not been governed rarely become a usable knowledge base — answers may look correct but fail in real use with gaps, inconsistent definitions and unclear permissions. Solutions usually start at the data governance layer.

Who maintains the system after delivery?

An AI project is not finished at launch; the knowledge base, prompts, workflows, permissions and cost rules need ongoing adjustment from feedback. SKY provides long-term AI coaching to help organizations build their own operating capability.
