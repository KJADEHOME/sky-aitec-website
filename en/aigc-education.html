FAQ

Education partnership FAQ

How can colleges and training providers launch AIGC courses and education agents?

We usually start with curriculum co-development: first align the program catalog and training goals, then set up the studio environment on the SkyVido creation module with education agents and knowledge bases in place, and finally create an outcome channel through real projects and competitions. Each step can begin as a small pilot before scaling.

How does SKY's education solution differ from generic AI tool vendors?

SKY does not build generic tools; we go deep on two verticals — culture and entertainment, and institutional education. Our solution combines commercial know-how in industrial content with experience delivering industry-education programs in universities, and we own an AIGC film competition IP, enabling customized, full-chain solutions for the sector.

Can institutions without compute or an AI foundation still run courses?

Yes. In the five-part framework, compute and tools are provided by the solution side, teaching staff build capability through training and coaching, and course content is tiered to learners' level.
