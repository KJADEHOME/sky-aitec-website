Company Positioning

SKY CULTURE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED is a culture and technology company focused on applied AIGC. The company does not build general-purpose AI tools; it works deeply in two vertical segments — entertainment content and college education — delivering industry-customized end-to-end solutions spanning AIGC content creation, digital humans, knowledge bases and data governance, with mature production delivery capability.

SKY combines hands-on industrial content commercialization with university industry-education deployment experience, and owns its own AIGC film competition IP. It serves three business directions: AI industry-education integration, industrialized AI entertainment creation, and AI-enabled government & enterprise cost reduction and efficiency.

Regional Layout

The company has built a three-region collaborative layout of "Hong Kong – Shanghai West Bund – Guangxi": Hong Kong serves as the international brand headquarters, connecting global technology resources and serving outbound, compliance and rights scenarios; Shanghai West Bund hosts the core AIGC R&D center within an AI industry cluster, focused on vertical industry models and technology iteration; Guangxi builds an ASEAN-facing AIGC digital content base radiating from the Nanning hub into creative, education and cross-border industry collaboration.

Milestones

  • Assembled the initial team in Nanning and set up the R&D center in Shanghai, securing early positions in the vertical entertainment and training tracks.
  • Matured and deployed the full-chain industrialized AIGC creation system, with university AI industry-education projects launched in parallel.
  • Used Hong Kong as the international brand window to connect diverse technology resources, landed in the Shanghai West Bund AI industry cluster and built a local R&D team.
  • Developed proprietary vertical-track AIGC industry models, building dedicated technical competitiveness.
