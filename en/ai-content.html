FAQ

Content partnership FAQ

Compared with traditional production, how much cost and time can AI short drama save?

With AI, short drama production cycles can shrink to weeks, capturing the market quickly; AI generates characters and scenes, removing high talent fees and crew overhead; and data-driven creation wins attention with fresh narrative and form. The exact ratio depends on genre and scale — bring us your project brief.

After a digital human project is delivered, how does ongoing operation work?

A digital human is an IP, and an IP is a core digital asset. SKY's method is persona design — an independent persona and identity matrix — plus commercial operation: full-frequency exposure, event ignition, content compounding, and absolute ownership, with exclusive rights, zero scandal risk and 100% controllability and iterability.

What scale can a culture and tourism film start from?

You can start small with a single film or a single-scenic-area clip to validate, then expand into a series. The AIGC workflow's advantage is reusability: visual assets and a narrative framework set once keep producing multilingual, multi-platform versions.
