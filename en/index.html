FAQ

Frequently asked

What does SKY CULTURE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED do?

SKY CULTURE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED is a Hong Kong culture and technology company focused on applied AIGC. Built on our own AIGC pipeline and the SkyVido platform, we provide solutions across three directions: AI in vocational education, industrialized AI content production, and enterprise AI for cost and efficiency.

What is the AI creation module?

It is our AIGC training capability module for education, covering copy and planning, graphic design, 3D design, short drama generation, infinite canvas and music composition — used for AIGC skills training, co-developed curricula and industry-education projects.

Which clients do you work with?

Government bodies, colleges and universities, training providers, culture, tourism and creative institutions, brands and marketing agencies, and enterprises building AI content and digital capability.

Can SKY build AI agents, RAG knowledge bases and private model deployments?

Yes. We deliver custom AI agents, data governance, RAG knowledge bases and orchestration platforms, multimodal model applications, private deployment of large models, and long-term AI coaching.

Which films has SKY produced in-house?

Our in-house productions are the “Baili Liujiang” Digital Boat Companion in Liuzhou (launched February 2026) and the Nanning city and industry promotion film (full AIGC production, 4K delivery). We also took part in the 2026 Year of the Horse AI Spring Festival Gala as a participant and founded the film & TV track of the 4th Shanghai International AIGC Innovation Competition.
