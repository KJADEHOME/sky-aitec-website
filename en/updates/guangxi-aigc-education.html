Nanning Gongda Robotics Base and SKY Shanghai: a cross-region breakthrough in AIGC industry-education integration

When AIGC enters education, the point is not just tool demos. It is about linking curriculum, hands-on training, real projects and industry collaboration into one system.

Use cases: vertical industry-education integration, AIGC talent development, hands-on training, cross-region collaboration.

When colleges and local industry bases start talking about AIGC, the conversation should not stop at buying a tool or running a one-off experience class. Real, deployable industry-education integration has to answer three questions at once: what students learn, how teachers teach, and how industry projects enter the training environment.

The collaboration between Nanning Gongda Robotics Base in Guangxi and SKY Shanghai is built around exactly this question. It is not a single-point tech showcase, but a cross-region path: SKY's AIGC R&D and content production in Shanghai connects to Guangxi's industry and education scenarios facing ASEAN, closing a sustainable loop between AI education, digital content and regional cooperation.

From training courses to real projects

The core of AIGC education is not just getting students to use a few popular tools, but helping them understand the full workflow — from topic selection, planning and copywriting to visual generation, video production and final delivery. For colleges, this means shifting courses from knowledge delivery to task-driven learning. For training providers, it means tying training to job competencies, portfolio outcomes and project retrospectives.

SKY's focus is vertical industry-education integration in AI, built around AIGC talent development, curriculum R&D, hands-on teaching programs, education agents, knowledge base development and training base construction. By bringing real content tasks into the classroom, students do more than learn AI tools — they build demonstrable, assessable and reviewable capabilities through project-based training.

Why cross-region collaboration is needed

Industry-education integration in AIGC naturally draws on multiple resources: an R&D center drives technology iteration, colleges and training providers handle talent development, and local industry bases own the application scenarios and project delivery. SKY CULTURE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED now operates a three-region layout across Hong Kong, Shanghai West Bund and the Guangxi ASEAN gateway — precisely so that technology, content and regional resources connect with one another.

For Guangxi and its ASEAN-facing digital content cooperation, AIGC can serve education and training, cultural tourism promotion, cultural and creative content production and cross-border digital distribution. For colleges, such cooperation extends courses from the classroom into industry projects. For enterprises and local institutions, it moves talent development and content production capabilities to the front of the project pipeline.

Who should collaborate

  • Colleges looking to build AIGC courses, training bases or industry-education integration programs.
  • Training providers wanting to introduce AI curriculum R&D, hands-on teaching and education agent capability.
  • Local industry bases aiming to build cooperation around AIGC cultural creation, cultural tourism promotion and outbound digital content.
  • Enterprises seeking to raise organizational digital capability through AI content production and talent training.

Seen this way, AIGC industry-education integration is neither a single course nor a one-off training, but a capability system that keeps running around talent, content, projects and regional collaboration. SKY's role is to turn AIGC technology into solutions that education institutions, industry bases and enterprise clients can actually deploy.
