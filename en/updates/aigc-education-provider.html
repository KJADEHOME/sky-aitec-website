Use case | AIGC Education

Choosing an AIGC training provider · from curriculum to deployed agents

Institutions building AIGC capability need more than tools — they must build curriculum, hands-on training, agents and operating capability.

Use case: AIGC curriculum development, AI education agents, training bases, industry-education integration, teacher training.

When colleges or training providers start building AIGC courses, the most common question is not whether they have tools, but how those tools enter teaching. If course goals, knowledge bases, hands-on projects, teacher training and student assessment are not designed together, AIGC easily stalls at the level of a trial or demo class.

Start with the curriculum, not the tool

A strong AIGC training provider needs to connect curriculum development, case material, teaching workflow and hands-on tasks. The curriculum must help students understand the basic capabilities of generative AI, and also let them master content production, prompt design, agent collaboration, knowledge base use and project delivery methods.

Education agents extend teaching capacity

AIGC education agents suit tasks such as pre-class preview, document Q&A, assignment coaching, project breakdown and teaching feedback. They do not replace teachers; they turn repetitive coaching, material organization and practice support into a sustainable teaching infrastructure.

Industry-education integration needs a real project entry point

For colleges building AI industry-education integration, the provider must understand both teaching and industry. Course content should connect to real industry scenarios — such as digital content production, brand marketing, culture & tourism promotion, enterprise knowledge bases, AI agents and multimodal content creation.

SKY's education direction

SKY CULTURE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED serves colleges and training providers with AIGC education agent R&D and implementation, smart-education AI application solutions, AIGC content production and training system building, curriculum development and hands-on project design.

For institutions that want to build AIGC education capability, SKY emphasizes a "curriculum + agent + hands-on training + operations" combination rather than a single-tool delivery. This path fits long-term teaching, admissions showcases, school-enterprise cooperation and student project training better.
