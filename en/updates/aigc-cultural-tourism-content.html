Use case | AI content

AIGC content for culture and tourism · from campaign asset to brand asset

AIGC can lift content production efficiency for culture and tourism institutions, but more importantly it builds a sustainable brand expression.

Use case: AI promotion films, digital humans, brand IP, short-video material, cross-border digital content.

Culture and tourism institutions face rising content demands: events need promotion, projects need investment attraction, scenic areas need refreshed material, and cities and parks need a more recognizable brand expression. AIGC's value is not only lower production cost, but turning content production from one-off projects into an ongoing capability.

An AI promotion film is not simple auto-generation

An effective AI promotion film must first define audience, scenario, narrative rhythm and communication goal. AIGC can lift the efficiency of script, storyboard, visuals, voiceover and multi-version adaptation, but it still needs content planning and brand judgment.

Digital humans and brand IP must serve the business

Digital humans, virtual hosts, brand IP and AI guides fit exhibition halls, events, short videos, courses and client communication. Their core is not "novelty", but helping institutions form a stable, consistent and reusable way of expression.

Digital content capability toward ASEAN

For Guangxi and ASEAN-facing culture and tourism projects, digital content also involves cross-language, cross-culture and cross-platform distribution. AIGC can support multilingual content drafts, material version expansion and regionalized distribution plans.

SKY's content production direction

SKY CULTURE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED serves culture, creative and entertainment institutions and enterprise clients with AIGC content production, AI entertainment creation, digital humans, brand IP, digital marketing and ASEAN-facing digital content cooperation plans.

SKY's content services emphasize a "content planning + AIGC toolchain + brand expression + training operations" combination, so institutions can complete current projects while gradually building their own AI content production capability.
